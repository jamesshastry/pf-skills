- **TX levies no income tax, so the Treasury exemption is worth nothing here.** Compare these purely on gross yield and convenience. The exemption is the durable part of the argument in a high-tax state and simply absent in a no-tax one — which is why the state belongs in the facts file rather than being assumed.

- Keep the emergency fund liquid and boring. Chasing yield with money that has to be available on a bad day defeats the purpose of holding it — this is about not leaving free yield on the table, not about taking risk with the buffer.

## Before acting

- **Enter yields net of expenses** — the SEC 7-day yield already is. Subtracting an expense ratio from a quoted yield double-counts it.
- **Check redemption fees.** A per-withdrawal charge makes a fund the wrong home for cash you actually draw on, whatever it yields.
- **Confirm settlement.** A placed order is not a held position, and a fund strikes at the next NAV.
